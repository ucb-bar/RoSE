  105 B at 10.10 c/B. The blowup is not in the transpose loop.

  gem_tb32 shifts -12.4% on n2h and +17.2% on h2n between arm A and arm E,
  UNIFORMLY across every shape and every C -- a constant factor on the inner
  loop, not a cache-set effect (that would be shape-dependent). Its POOLED cost
  is unchanged (7.03 -> 7.05). The RVV kernels do not move at all: their
  per-direction values are identical to two decimals in all four arms.

  Consequence for the report: quote the scalar floor as a POOLED 7.03-7.05 c/B.
  Do NOT quote the scalar n2h-vs-h2n asymmetry as a property of the transpose;
  it is a property of the binary. The RVV asymmetry (n2h 4.14 vs h2n 2.86
  strided; 0.85 vs 0.90 segment) IS reproducible and can be quoted.
